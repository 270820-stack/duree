This work, "Durée" is French for duration, a philosophical term coined by Henri Bergson to explore the continuation of time as always mobile and incomplete. Digits from 1 to 9, representing time, fall like sand into nine invisible clocks. Each hand turns at its own speed, gathering numbers, then sweeping them away, this represents different aspects of daily life, and passage of time in the unnoticed measures. Time seeps through in all seen and unseen corners, but one could not divert their attention to all of them, so some/even most of time is destined to be lost. This work imagines time to be infinite, so the process of preserving time becomes numb, and eventually accepting it and simply letting it flow.
